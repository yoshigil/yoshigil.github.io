Volcanic Vortex Ring Rise Model

A 1D variable-entrainment vortex-ring / thermal rise model for natural volcanic vortex rings, adapted for a real stratified atmosphere. Runs entirely in your browser (via Pyodide/WebAssembly) — nothing is sent to a server. Source: Johan Gilchrist.

Research Highlights

Dynamics Governing the Delivery of SO2 to the Atmosphere by Volcanic Plumes

PAGES Magazine · October 2026 · Vol. 34 No. 2, pp. 60–61

Can the largest explosive eruptions (M ≥8) cause global volcanic winters? Growing evidence suggests not as intensely as once assumed. We highlight how eruption dynamics control the separation of erupted material between buoyant plumes and pyroclastic density currents, SO2 scavenging by ash and water, and rapid ash–sulfate aggregation and fallout to limit stratospheric sulfur injection and help reconcile discrepancies between petrologic and ice-core sulfur records.
